# Trend & Setups (Phases 2–3): specification, evidence, hypotheses

- **Indicator:** `pine/trend_setups.pine` (price overlay).
- **Strategy version:** `pine/trend_setups_strategy.pine`. It is generated by `research/tools/build_strategy.py`; never edit it by hand.
- **Python twin:** `research/tfs/trend.py`.
- **Tests:** `research/tests/test_trend.py`, `research/tests/test_build_strategy.py`.
- **Status:** built Nov 2026. All 44 project tests pass. **Not yet compiled in TradingView**, and predictive value **not yet tested** (Phase 4).

## 1. What it answers

| Question | Module |
|---|---|
| Is there a trend, and how strong is it relative to noise? | Calibrated trend score and regime |
| Do the higher timeframe and the market agree? | Context regime (non-repainting) and relative strength vs SPY |
| Where is the trade idea wrong? | Confirmed swings, last swing high/low, anchored VWAP |
| When to enter, where to stop, how much to buy? | Setups S1–S3, initial stop, trailing stop, position size |

## 2. Definitions

**Trend score.** Three EMA crossovers of log price: (8,24), (16,48), (32,96) bars. These are the published defaults of Baz et al. (2015) and are not tuned.
- Each crossover is divided by σ·c_k. Here σ is the volatility of returns (EWMA of squared returns, span 100, previous bars only), and c_k = 1.4179 / 2.0013 / 2.8289 is the exact standard deviation of that crossover under a random walk.
- The three z-scores are averaged and divided by 0.9171, the exact standard deviation of the average.
- **Result:** without a trend the score is N(0, 1), so a score of 2 means the same thing on every stock and timeframe. With shuffled returns the measured standard deviation is 0.97–0.98.

**Regime (hysteresis).** Up when score ≥ +1.0; back to neutral when it falls below +0.3; down when ≤ −1.0; back to neutral above −0.3. "Strong" when |score| ≥ 2.

**Context.** The same regime on a higher timeframe (Auto: 1h→4h, 4h→D, D→W, W→M). It uses the last *completed* higher-timeframe bar, so it never repaints.

**Relative strength.** The same engine applied to ln(close ÷ benchmark close). The benchmark defaults to SPY.

**Swings.** A swing high is confirmed when the close falls 2 ATR below the highest high of the up-leg; swing lows mirror this. Labels are HH/LH and HL/LL. The label is drawn at the swing bar *once the swing is confirmed*; signals use only the confirmation bar.

**Anchored VWAP.** Computed from the last confirmed swing low while the trend is not down, and from the last swing high in a downtrend.

**Extension.** (close − baseline) ÷ ATR14. The baseline is a two-pole smoother with a 50-bar equivalent window.

**Relative volume.** The same as in Volume Flow v2: normalised per time-of-day slot on intraday charts, over 50 bars on D/W/M.

## 3. Setups (long side)

All setups need at least 200 bars of history and act on the close of the signal bar. Only one position is open at a time.

**S1 Pullback continuation** (primary)
- **Context:** trend up.
- **Arms when:** the close comes within 0.25 ATR of the baseline (or below it).
- **Trigger:** a close above the prior bar's high, provided:
  - the trend is still up and the last swing low was not broken;
  - average relative volume during the pullback was ≤ 1.0;
  - the context is not down, and relative strength is not down.
- **Initial stop:** pullback low − 0.25 ATR.

**S2 Compression breakout**
- **Context:** trend not down; context not down; relative strength not down.
- **Arms when:** the prior bar had ATR14 ≤ 0.8 × ATR100.
- **Trigger:** a close above the prior 20-bar high on relative volume ≥ 1.5.
- **Initial stop:** 10-bar low − 0.25 ATR.

**S3 Confirmed reversal** (half size)
- **Context:**
  - the trend was down within the last 20 bars;
  - extension reached ≤ −2 ATR in that window;
  - the trend is not up.
- **Trigger:** a close above the last swing high (structure break) with score > −0.3.
- **Initial stop:** last swing low − 0.25 ATR.

**Exits.** Close below max(initial stop, highest high since entry − 3 ATR), or the trend turns down. Exits are close-based, the same as entries.

**Size.** Account size × risk % ÷ (entry − stop). S3 uses half size. The strategy version sizes from live strategy equity.

## 4. E04 results (smoke test: three daily series, not evidence)

| | S&P 500 | NASDAQ | GOOG (vs SPX) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trend score sd: real / shuffled | 0.85 / 0.98 | 0.90 / 0.97 | 1.04 / 0.97 |
| Time up / neutral / down | 33 / 54 / 13% | 41 / 43 / 16% | 36 / 50 / 14% |
| Regime switches/yr (VFI zero crosses/yr) | 4.7 (7.5) | 4.7 (6.4) | 4.1 (6.3) |
| Long only in up-trend: Sharpe / max DD | 0.04 / −22% | 0.37 / −18% | 0.55 / −22% |
| Buy & hold: Sharpe / max DD | 0.26 / −57% | 0.19 / −78% | 0.50 / −65% |
| Trades (per year) | 16 (0.9) | 27 (1.4) | 10 (1.4) |
| Average R, all trades | −0.16 | +0.47 | +0.71 |

**What the results mean:**

- **Calibration holds.** The score behaves as N(0,1) when there is no trend.
- **No excess trendiness.** On the 2000–2018 indices the score's spread was *below* the random-walk value, so at 8–96-bar horizons these indices were not trendier than a random walk in that period.
- **Drawdowns.** The regime filter's demonstrated property is drawdown control (max DD −18% to −22% instead of −57% to −78%). It did not consistently add return.
- **Setups.** The trade counts are too small to judge (the standard error of the average R is about ±0.4). S2 never fired on indices: aggregate index volume rarely reaches 1.5× normal.
- **Nothing was tuned** on this data.

Every hypothesis is subject to `02_research_protocol.md`: development / validation / lockbox, a ticker split, a trial registry, PBO < 0.3, and DSR.

| ID | Hypothesis | Test | Accept if |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | The regime filter improves risk-adjusted returns of single stocks | Per-stock Sharpe and max DD, regime-filtered vs buy & hold and vs close > SMA200 | Better in ≥ 60% of stocks and in ≥ 2/3 of sub-periods; DD reduction significant |
| H3 | S1 entries beat random entries taken in the same up-trends | Expectancy in R vs matched random entries (same stock, regime and holding time) | Difference > 0 with 95% CI in development and validation |
| H8 | S2 breakouts have positive expectancy after costs | Average R with bootstrap CI | CI > 0 in validation |

| H10 | The context and RS filters improve S1/S2 | Same setups with each filter on vs off | Expectancy gain with CI > 0; not merely fewer trades |

## 6. Known limitations

- **Long side only.** Bearish structure breaks and trend-down alerts are for managing longs.
- **No earnings awareness yet.** Check the earnings date yourself before entering.
- **Close-based stops.** They are simpler and consistent between indicator and strategy, but real stop orders fill intrabar. The strategy tester therefore shows close-to-close fills.
- **Untested in TradingView.**
  - The scripts parse (pynescript) but have not been compiled.
  - The multi-timeframe table uses `request.security` with lookahead on the previous bar, the standard non-repainting form.
  - If the benchmark symbol name differs on your data feed, change it in the settings (e.g. `SPY` or `AMEX:SPY`).

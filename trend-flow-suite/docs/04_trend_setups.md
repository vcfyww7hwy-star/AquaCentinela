# Trend & Setups v2: specification and evidence

- **Indicator:** `pine/trend_setups.pine` (price overlay; title "Trend & Setups v2").
- **Strategy version:** `pine/trend_setups_strategy.pine`. Generated by `research/tools/build_strategy.py`; never edit it by hand.
- **Python twin:** `research/tfs/trend.py`. Tests: `research/tests/test_trend.py`, `test_build_strategy.py`.
- **Status:** rebuilt 10 Nov 2026 from the tests in `06_validation_lite.md`. 94 project tests pass. **Not yet compiled in TradingView.**

## 1. What changed from v1, and why

v1 assumed that a trend state, pullback and breakout entries, and a few filters would give an edge.
Tested on about 1.6 million stock-days (1999–2018), none of them did. v2 keeps what the evidence
supports and labels the rest.

| v1 | v2 | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Chart-timeframe trend as the main state | **Long-term (higher-timeframe) trend** as the main state, used as a risk switch | The trend state does not predict returns; standing aside while the weekly trend is down cut drawdowns by 29–40% at the same Sharpe ratio (E09) |
| S1: pullback, then wait for a close above the prior high | **D: dip in a long-term uptrend**, bought at the dip's close | S1 was no better than random entries. Dips in uptrends bounce for 1–5 bars (confirmed on an independent 14-year set) |
| S2 breakout, S3 reversal on by default | Off by default, labelled | No edge; S3 significantly negative on the long set |
| Context, strength, pullback-volume filters on | Removed, or optional and off | No reliable effect |
| Exits on the close | **Stop orders** (intrabar), with a position cap | About 1% of trades worse than −1.5R, against 5–9% |
| Signals hidden while a simulated trade was open | Setups are detected on every bar | Usability |
| Volatility from plain squared returns | Winsorised volatility | One earnings gap no longer distorts the trend score for months |

## 2. Definitions

**Trend score.** Three EMA crossovers of log price: (8,24), (16,48), (32,96) bars (Baz et al. 2015,
not tuned). Each is divided by σ·c_k, with c_k = 1.4179 / 2.0013 / 2.8289 the exact standard
deviation of that crossover under a random walk. The average is divided by 0.9171. Without a trend
the score is N(0, 1).

**Volatility σ.** EWMA of squared returns, span 100, previous bars only. After the first 100 bars
each squared return is capped at (3σ)². A 30σ gap then moves σ by at most 8%, instead of roughly
quadrupling it.

**Trend state.** Up at score ≥ +1.0, back to neutral below +0.3; Down at ≤ −1.0, back to neutral
above −0.3. "Strong" at |score| ≥ 2.

**Long-term trend.** The same state on a higher timeframe, taken from the last *completed*
higher-timeframe bar (no repainting). Auto: charts below 4h → Daily; 4h and Daily → Weekly;
Weekly → Monthly. Only Daily-with-Weekly is tested.

**Market and strength.** The same engine on the benchmark's close (market trend) and on
ln(close ÷ benchmark) (relative strength). Information only.

**5-bar move.** (ln C − ln C[5]) ÷ (σ·√5): the last five bars' return in risk units.

**Structure.** Swing highs and lows confirmed by a 2-ATR close reversal; labels HH/HL/LH/LL drawn at
the swing bar once confirmed. Optional anchored VWAP from the last swing.

## 3. Setups (long side)

Setups are detected on every closed bar after 200 bars of history, whether or not a trade is open.

| Setup | Rule | Initial stop | Default |
|---|---|---|---|
| **D** dip in a long-term uptrend | Long-term trend Up, and the 5-bar move ≤ −1.5σ on the first bar of the dip | Close − 2 ATR | On |
| **B** compression breakout | Chart trend not down; prior bar ATR14 ≤ 0.8 × ATR100; close above the prior 20-bar high on relative volume ≥ 1.5 | 10-bar low − 0.25 ATR, at least 1 ATR away | Off |
| **R** confirmed reversal (half size) | Chart trend down within 20 bars and extension ≤ −2 ATR in that window; close above the last swing high; score > −0.3 | Last swing low − 0.25 ATR, at least 1 ATR away | Off |

Optional filter, off by default and untested for D: D and B need the market trend not down.

## 4. The tracked trade

One long position is tracked at a time; a setup that fires while a trade is open is shown as a dot.

- **Entry:** the close of the signal bar.
- **Stop:** a stop order at the pink line. The script checks each bar's low against the level set at
  the previous close; after a gap through the stop the exit is the open. The level is moved up to the
  highest high since entry − 3 ATR and never moves down. "On the close" is available as an option.
- **Trend exit:** at the close when the trend behind the trade turns Down. For D that is the
  long-term trend; for B and R, the chart trend.
- **Time exit (optional):** at the close N bars after entry.
- **Size:** account × risk % ÷ (entry − stop), capped at 25% of the account; R uses half. The
  strategy version sizes from live strategy equity.
- **"If I buy this close"** in the table applies the same stop (2 ATR) and size rules to the current
  bar, for entries you choose yourself.

## 5. Evidence for the default setup (rule D)

Daily bars, long-term trend = weekly. R-multiples before costs; brackets are 95% intervals.

| Set | Forward 5-bar return vs uptrend bars without a dip (risk units) | Trades with the default trailing stop | Random entries in the same uptrends |
|---|---|---|---|
| A, 231 S&P stocks, 2014–18 (where the rule was found) | +0.20 [+0.02, +0.35] | +0.17R [−0.03, +0.35], 37% winners | +0.10R (p = 0.05) |
| B, 258 other S&P stocks, 2014–18 | +0.18 [+0.03, +0.33] | +0.13R [−0.05, +0.31], 38% | +0.09R (p = 0.05) |
| DJ, 31 Dow-set stocks, 2007–13 | +0.06 [−0.16, +0.27] | +0.10R [−0.20, +0.43], 38% | +0.05R (p = 0.33) |
| LH, 459 stocks, 1999–2013 (independent) | **+0.12 [+0.03, +0.20]** | +0.09R [−0.03, +0.21], 37% | +0.07R (p = 0.05) |

**How to read this:**

- The bounce after a dip in an uptrend is real, small (about +0.3% to +0.5% over five bars for a
  typical stock) and short (gone by bar 10 on the long set).
- As a complete trade rule the average result is about +0.1R with an interval that includes zero.
  It beat random entries on A, B and LH (p = 0.05 each), not on DJ. It lost money in 2001, 2002 and 2008.
- About 2 to 2.5 signals per stock per year on daily charts; trades last about 17 bars.
- The frozen confirmation criteria were not met (`06_validation_lite.md`, sections 2.4–2.5), so D
  ships as an **unconfirmed candidate**: a timing aid for buying strength-in-weakness inside
  uptrends, not a system.

## 6. Known limitations

- **Long side only.**
- **Daily bars, large US stocks, 1999–2018.** Nothing is tested on 1h, 4h or weekly charts, on small
  caps, or on other markets. The datasets hold survivors, so absolute returns are overstated.
- **Gaps.** A stop order does not protect against a gap; the worst tested trade lost 21R. The
  position cap and diversification are the defence.
- **Earnings.** The table shows the next earnings date from TradingView (live only). It is not used
  in the rules or in backtests.
- **Untested in TradingView.** The scripts pass a syntax parse only. `earnings.future_time` and the
  multi-timeframe requests are the likeliest places for a compile error.
